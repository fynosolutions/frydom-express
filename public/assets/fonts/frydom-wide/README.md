# Frydom body fonts

OFL-1.1 derivatives of the existing Mouse Memoirs Latin and Smiley Sans fonts.
Generated by `scripts/widen-body-fonts.py` with horizontal outlines, advances,
bearings and positioning adjustments scaled to 110%; vertical metrics unchanged.
Renamed to Frydom Display Wide and Frydom Han Wide to respect reserved names.
Upstream copyright and license notices are retained in the fonts and adjacent files.
