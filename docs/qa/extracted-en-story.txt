MADE WITH CARE
Fresh food.
Full-on flavor.
We use carefully selected chicken, fry to order, and rely on a coating technique designed to deliver an even golden crunch while keeping the chicken juicy inside.
Our flavor system pairs classic fried chicken with globally inspired sauces and seasonings, from honey mustard and sweet chili to Korean-style heat and dry spice blends.
