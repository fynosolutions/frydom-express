Why Frydom Express

We focus on one thing and do it well: fried chicken that delivers crunch, flavor, and comfort in every order.

  • Quality chicken and fresh ingredients, fried to order for a crisp outside and juicy bite.
  • Our signature seasoned coating, paired with globally inspired dipping sauces, gives classic fried chicken deeper, more memorable flavor.
  • Open-kitchen service and neighborhood-friendly stores built around fresh food and a welcoming experience.
