Our Story

Starting in Brooklyn, Frydom Express was built around one simple goal: serve fried chicken that feels fresh, flavorful, and worth coming back for.

We use carefully selected chicken, fry to order, and rely on a coating technique designed to deliver an even golden crunch while keeping the chicken juicy inside.

Our flavor system pairs classic fried chicken with globally inspired sauces and seasonings, from honey mustard and sweet chili to Korean-style heat and dry spice blends.

Golden Crunch · Juicy Bite Signature Seasoned Coating · Global Sauces Brooklyn Roots · Neighborhood Appeal
Food

Crunch is the first impression

We fry fresh in store so the coating stays crisp while the chicken stays juicy. Our signature seasoned coating and craveable sauces give every order a more distinctive flavor profile.
Experience

Bright, clean, and welcoming

Our stores are designed to feel warm and approachable, with an open kitchen and a layout that works for both dine-in guests and fast takeout orders.
Operations

Consistent standards with local fit

We keep core product standards consistent while leaving room to adjust selected flavors and menu mixes so each store feels right for its neighborhood.
